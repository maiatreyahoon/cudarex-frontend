The submission folder contains a draft description, timed recording script, judge Q&A, checklist and pitch material. Keep all statements consistent with the actual app. Explain significant changes to any earlier project accurately. Never represent mocks, scripted fallback, unpublished links or unperformed model calls as finished external work.

The four published judging areas are implementation, design, impact and quality of idea. The product's proposed case is specific: a student can retain chosen context, practice through attempts, and review assistant actions in one inspectable workspace. Demonstrate that case and its limits rather than making unsupported scale or adoption claims.
